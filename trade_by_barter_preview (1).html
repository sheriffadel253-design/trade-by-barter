How it works

Make exchanges feel simple, transparent, and rewarding.

01

List what you have

Add a title, category, condition, location, photos, and what you want in exchange.

02

Propose a trade

Choose an item you'd like and explain what you're offering. The other party can review your proposal.

03

Exchange safely

Agree on item condition and exchange terms. Meet in a public place and inspect items before swapping.

Never share passwords or one-time codes. Be cautious of requests for unexpected fees.
